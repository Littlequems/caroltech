## Selected case studies

### 1. Spreadsheet-to-Report Workflow
A workflow for importing structured lists, keeping records tied to their source, supporting manual review/classification, validating completion, and producing standardized reports. The underlying prototype explicitly separates temporary import, manual review, final validation, and release to the downstream system.

**Commercial angle:** Excel/CSV intake → validation → classification → dashboard/report → export.

### 2. Financial Reconciliation / XpertShield
A demonstration workflow that compares transaction data from different sources, flags discrepancies, produces an audit report, and prepares corrected output. Demo data is synthetic.

**Commercial angle:** Import → reconcile → flag exceptions → review → report/export.

### 3. Small Business Operations / Inove Studio
A management system for service businesses combining scheduling, customers, vehicles, quotes, work orders, products/materials, cash controls, images, and history. Public demo uses fictional data.

**Commercial angle:** Replace fragmented spreadsheets/messages with one lightweight operations dashboard.
